# Source Register 2026

This is the canonical provenance register for fiscal claims in [Fiscal Rule Catalog 2026](fiscal-rules-2026.md). Primary legislation and competent official institutions are canonical; secondary material may support discovery or non-canonical corroboration only.

## Admissibility

A source entry must establish every relevant dimension of the claim: year/effective period, jurisdiction, profile applicability and exclusions, base, rate or bracket, threshold semantics, ordering/interactions, and rounding. A source containing a rate without its base or applicability is insufficient.

## Required record fields

Each entry owns: stable Source ID; issuer; title; official URL; document type; publication date when available; effective date when distinct; fiscal year; jurisdiction; access date; precise article/section/page/paragraph where possible; concise supported claim; authority notes; and supersession/conflict notes.
